Nicholas Buhmann-Holmes

Research fellow
Lund University

I am a research fellow at Lund University. My research focuses on party politics from different angles: I study the evolution of party unity, how parties and politicians behave in parliamentary debates, and how parties exploit events for political advantage. My work heavily engages with political developments across one or more decades that I analyze to advance knowledge about our current age. My work focuses on quantitative approaches and large langeuage model approaches that I use to study both historical and more recent material. I have a particular interest in Scandinavia.

My research has been published in Comparative Political Studies, Journal of European Public Policy and Scandinavian Political Studies. Moreover, I have received funding from Carlsbergfondet for my research.

As part of my research, I have built a dataset of all parliamentary debates from the Danish Folketinget, spanning 1910-2024. If you would like to use this data, feel free to contact me.

I am also an avid follower of football and cycling, and I have a great interest in music, literature and film. And, of course, I follow everything that has to with politics - whether work related or not.

Data

I have built a dataset of all speeches delivered in the Folketinget, the Danish parliament, spanning 1910–2024.

The dataset contains the full text of parliamentary speeches, together with information on the date of the speech, speaker, and political party.

If you are interested in using the dataset, please get in touch.
