Nicholas Buhmann-Holmes

Research fellow
Lund University

I am a research fellow at Lund University. My research focuses on party politics from different angles: I study the evolution of party unity, I study how parties and politicians behave in parliamentary debates, and I study how parties exploit events for political advantage. I have a particular interest in Scandinavia.

My research has been published in Comparative Political Studies, Journal of European Public Policy and Scandinavian Political Studies. Moreover, I have received funding from Carlsbergfondet for my research.

As part of my research, I have built a dataset of all parliamenteary debates from the Danish Folketinget, spanning 1910-2024. If you would like to use this data, feel free to contact me.

I am also an avid follower of football and cycling, and I have a great interest in music, literature and film. And, of course, I follow everything that has to with politics - whether work related or not.
